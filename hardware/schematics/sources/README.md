# Schematic source figures

> **Historical Rev A source figures.** These depict physical aircraft from the superseded design. They are not parts to buy or build for the active project: controller simulation in Simantic, then a physical controller prototype, followed by virtual drone electronics/microcontroller outputs driving an on-screen mechanical simulation. Pocket/ELRS/trainer hardware is not required. Use the [project goals](../../../README.md) and [current architecture](../../../docs/architecture.md).

These original, unmodified manufacturer figures were retrieved for the Rev A research on 14 September 2026 and copied from the project's existing research artifacts. They are the two raster figures used by the [PDF builder](../../../tools/build_palmpilot_schematic.py). The editable wiring sheets are authored separately.
