Assumed IMU mounting: breakout flat on the back of the hand, its x axis toward the fingers and z out of the back (the firmware takes roll from y, which becomes yaw on this hand). Heading cannot be seen from gravity, so the hands never turn left or right here. Before calibration, bend is estimated from the straightest reading seen so far and about 700 counts of travel.
