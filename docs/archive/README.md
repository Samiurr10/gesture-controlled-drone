# Historical architecture archive

**Scope changed 2026-09-16.** The active goals are (1) simulate and validate the hand controller in Simantic, then build its physical prototype, and (2) model the drone hardware/microcontroller in Simantic and feed its actuator signals into an on-screen mechanical simulation. Physical drone construction and flight tests are outside scope. Pocket, ELRS and trainer hardware are not required.
